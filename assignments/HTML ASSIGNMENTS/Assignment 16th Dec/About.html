Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

Name: Dr. Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam
Born: 15 October 1931, Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu
Profession: Aerospace Scientist
Known As: Missile Man of India
Position: 11th President of India
Awards: Bharat Ratna, Padma Bhushan, Padma Vibhushan


Education

Dr. Kalam studied physics at St. Joseph's College, Tiruchirappalli.
He later specialized in aerospace engineering from the Madras Institute of Technology.
His education laid the foundation for India's missile and space programs.
